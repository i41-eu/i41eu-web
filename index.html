i41

An open-source home platform: local first, privacy first.

Your robots, devices and apps work at home, on your own network. The online part helps you set things up and reach them from away, and runs some apps on its servers; where end-to-end encryption is on, it is built so that it cannot read what it relays. The first device is a Stackchan robot.
