A focus timer for your desk: the robot is the timer, a phone optional.

Open focus.q41.eu See it on the 3D robot Set up a robot

On the robot

25 minutes of focus, a 5 minute break, and a longer break after every four. No phone is needed: tap the robot's screen or its head to start or pause, hold the screen to skip.

  • The screen: the time left as a ring and as digits, today's count, and a line for the room: "FREE IN 12 MIN" while you focus, "STRETCH" on a break.
  • The lights: both LED strips show the time left as a shrinking bar, dim: red while focusing, green or blue on a break, amber when paused. A sign for others in the room.
  • At the end of a phase: a chime, and a nod after focus or a look up after a break. Each can be turned off.

A focus period cut short does not count, and nothing punishes it.

On the page

Pair a phone or computer with the robot's QR code for the rest: what you focus on, two marks for interruptions without stopping the timer, today's focus periods and minutes, days in a row, the last seven days, and the settings: 25/5, 50/10 or 90/20, a daily goal, when your day starts (for night owls), and the robot's chime, lights and moves.

Parts it uses

Every part Focus uses and what it does with it. Pick a part to see where it sits on the 3D robot.

  • Display The phase, the time left as a ring and as digits, today's count, the set's dots, and a line for the room ("FREE IN 12 MIN").
  • Touch screen Tap to start or pause, hold to skip to the next phase.
  • Head press A touch on the head starts or pauses, as a tap on the screen does.
  • RGB LEDs The time left as a shrinking bar: red while focusing, green or blue on a break, amber when paused, off when idle.
  • Speaker A chime at the end of a phase: a rising bell after focus, two calm notes after a break.
  • Head pitch servo A nod after focus, a look up after a break.

Start

  1. Set your robot up on m.i41.eu with Focus among its apps (one click over USB in Chrome or Edge).
  2. On the robot, swipe up for the app switcher and pick Focus, or switch it from the manager's page.
  3. Tap the robot to start. For the statistics, scan its QR code with your phone (swipe up, QR).
